# Drive Rule - Frontier

`drive = :frontier` — looks for an undeveloped theory sitting next to a
developed one: working the known edge outward, one bridging theory at a
time.

This is the rule closest to Kauffman/Loreto's "adjacent possible" — see
[[References]] — operationalized directly as a lattice-adjacency search
rather than left as a metaphor.

## Numbers (L=24)
- stationary density: 2.078
- activity: 0.416
- mean avalanche size ⟨s⟩: 57.0 — close to [[Drive Rule - Uniform]]
- τ (truncated power-law fit): 0.865

On a *finite* lattice every theory eventually gets worked, so after the
initial transient this rule degenerates into uniform driving (no frontier
left to prefer). [[Drive Rule - Novelty-Seeking]]'s theory-creation mechanic
was added specifically to address this limitation, by letting the space grow
instead of saturate.

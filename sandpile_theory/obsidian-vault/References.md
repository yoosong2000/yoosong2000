## Sandpiles & SOC — the core
- Bak, Tang, Wiesenfeld. *Self-organized criticality.* Phys. Rev. A 38, 364
  (1988). — [[Self-Organized Criticality]]
- Dhar. *Self-organized critical state of sandpile automaton models.* PRL
  64, 1613 (1990) — the Abelian property. — [[Bak-Tang-Wiesenfeld Sandpile]]
- Pruessner. *Self-Organised Criticality.* Cambridge (2012) — the reference
  work on measuring exponents correctly.

## Corrections worth knowing
- Jensen, Christensen, Fogedby. *1/f noise, distribution of lifetimes, and a
  pile of sand.* PRB 40, 7425 (1989) — BTW gives 1/f², not 1/f.
- Clauset, Shalizi, Newman. *Power-law distributions in empirical data.*
  SIAM Review 51, 661 (2009) — non-negotiable before claiming one. —
  [[Truncated Power-Law Fitting]]

## Science as the thing being modeled
- Kitcher. *The division of cognitive labor.* J. Philosophy 87, 5 (1990) —
  start here. — [[Social Epistemology of Credit]]
- Merton. *The Matthew effect in science.* Science 159, 56 (1968). —
  [[Drive Rule - Matthew Effect]]
- Weisberg, Muldoon. *Epistemic landscapes and the division of cognitive
  labor.* Phil. Sci. 76, 225 (2009) — the closest existing ABM to this one.
  — [[Social Epistemology of Credit]]
- Foster, Rzhetsky, Evans. *Tradition and innovation in scientists' research
  strategies.* ASR 80, 875 (2015) — measures the explore/exploit tradeoff
  [[Novelty-Seeking Phase Transition]] is about.
- Kauffman; Loreto et al. — the "adjacent possible," operationalized in
  [[Drive Rule - Frontier]].

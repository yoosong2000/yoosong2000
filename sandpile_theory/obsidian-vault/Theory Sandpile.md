# Theory Sandpile

A [[Bak-Tang-Wiesenfeld Sandpile]], re-read so a grid cell is a theory and a
grain is a scientist, built with Agents.jl. This note is the map of content —
start here and follow the links.

> [!abstract] One-line thesis
> A self-organized-criticality model where who-works-on-what is governed by a
> credit rule, used to ask whether a given incentive structure for scientists
> pushes the field toward or away from scale-free research cascades.
